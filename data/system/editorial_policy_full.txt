Horse Legal Guide is published by Wise Covington PLLC, a law firm built by equestrians for the equestrian community. This page sets out how the pages on this site are chosen, written, checked, dated and corrected.
What we publish. Horse Legal Guide publishes general legal education about equine law: horse sale contracts, boarding and lease agreements, liability waivers, equine business formation and the disputes that arise around them. It does not publish legal advice, and no page here creates an attorney-client relationship.
How a topic is chosen. Pages are built around questions horse owners, trainers, barns and equine businesses actually ask, not around keywords.
How a page is written. Each page answers its question directly, then separates the parties, documents, timing, money, possession, communications and state-specific facts rather than repeating a generic contract overview. Where an answer depends on your state, a deadline, a minor, an entity, disputed authority, injury or insurance, the page says so and routes the question to the firm instead of guessing.
How a page is approved. No page publishes without review and approval by the firm. Approval alone does not publish future-dated content before its scheduled date, and an approval can be revoked: a revoked page is taken off the site.
Dates. Editorial pages carry their publication date, and the structured data on every page carries the same dates a reader sees.
Corrections. If you believe a page is wrong or out of date, contact Wise Covington PLLC through https://wisecovington.com. A page found to be wrong is corrected or taken down, and its visible date is updated when its substance changes.
Independence. Pages are not sponsored, and no outside party pays to appear in or shape them.
